How your information is used

While you type, details remain in this page’s memory. Submitting sends only the displayed fields to Shevato and the named source. We do not save your forms to local storage or sync them to your account.

Every price comparison is answered entirely from published CMS data held on our own server, so nothing about your search is sent to an insurer, a plan, a marketplace or anyone else. Private results may remain in server memory for up to 15 minutes, or six hours for published plan rates that do not change during the plan year, scoped to a random key held only in this browser tab. NHTSA vehicle data may be cached separately for up to 30 days. Refresh requests new prices; source dates remain visible.

Usage counters protect provider access; they do not contain your form details. Quotes are not insurance advice, enrollment or binding. Rankings do not use commissions.
