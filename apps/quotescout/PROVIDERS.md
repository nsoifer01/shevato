# Provider research and launch requirements

Reviewed 7 September 2026 against the official sources below. Public documentation being reachable is evidence of a documented product, not proof that an authenticated endpoint or a Shevato partnership is operational. No partner has been contacted or represented as contracted by this implementation. Undisclosed pricing, limits and terms remain **unknown**, not "free" or "unlimited". No scraping is implemented.

### Medicare, researched and not implemented

Medicare Advantage and Part D landscape files carry real premiums by contract, plan, state and county, are published every autumn and have historically been free bulk downloads. They would be the natural way to serve people over 64, whom the marketplace dataset cannot price. They are not implemented here because the current download page could not be resolved to a stable URL on 7 September 2026: the CMS enrollment-data pages no longer link the landscape files, the archive that is linked covers CY2006-CY2025, and `www.medicare.gov`'s plan-compare API answers 403 to automated requests, which this project will not circumvent. This is a lead with a concrete next step, not a blocked vertical: find the current landscape-file URL, confirm the licence, and the existing dataset pipeline generalises to it.

## Insurance licensing boundary

[NAIC producer licensing](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/producer-licensing) and its [State Licensing Handbook](https://content.naic.org/state_licensing_handbook.htm) describe licensing around selling, soliciting and negotiating insurance. Rules and referral-compensation restrictions are jurisdiction-specific. A “comparison UI” label does not itself establish an exemption. Before activating auto/home or compensated insurance referrals, obtain state-specific legal review and partner approval; the partner must own regulated quoting/binding and required disclosures. This implementation does not establish that Shevato is a producer or that a particular business model is exempt.

CMS plan information access does not confer broker/enrollment authority. HealthCare.gov is a general plan-review destination, not a bindable checkout for a displayed Quote Scout estimate. Service contracts likewise need jurisdiction/administrator review rather than being casually called insurance or a manufacturer warranty.

## Production credential checklist

Nothing on this list is required for the health or dental comparisons that are live today; those need no credential at all.

- CMS API: issued key, rotation owner, observed account limits, verified current coverage year and county behavior. No public example key in production.
- EasyPost: approved platform agreement, production key, explicit carrier account IDs and permission to rate for these visitors; confirm per-call billing, minimal input acceptance, and account-specific availability. No test-mode rates.
- Ship.Cars: OAuth client/access credentials and provider entitlements, official response specification, broker permissions, sandbox, contractual TTL and purchase handoff.
- PCMI: selected administrator/product, API portal permissions and rating/contract credentials, dealer/consumer rights and administrator sandbox.
- Insurify or another licensed partner: executed agreement, scoped credentials, quoting versus indicative-pricing contract, progressive input schema, state/license coverage and allowed handoff URL rules.
- Internet/energy: explicit data-use rights, geographic/serviceability coverage, price freshness and tariff normalization before provider registration.

Do not create placeholder production endpoints for unknown partner contracts. Retry only documented idempotent operations. Do not invent a referral link, “verified” state, fee or expiration guarantee that the source does not supply. Our five/fifteen-minute TTLs are conservative display freshness policies, not promises to honor checkout pricing.
